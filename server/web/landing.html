Pick a game. Jump straight in.

Your installed VR games appear in the headset with their artwork. Choose one and RinCast starts SteamVR and the game on your PC, while hyperspace plays around you until the first frame arrives.

  • H.264, HEVC and AV1 on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics
  • 72, 80, 90 and 120 Hz, as far as your Quest goes
  • PC sound in the headset, your voice in the game

Away from home? Your PC comes along.

Sign in on both devices and your PC appears in the headset wherever you are. The account server only introduces them. Then the video goes straight from your PC to your headset, encrypted, and never through anyone else's computer.

  • Adapts to slow connections and recovers on its own
  • A PC only accepts headsets you've allowed on that PC
  • No account needed on your own network

If you don't like something, change it.

RinCast is MIT licensed, with no subscription, no ads and nothing held back. The streamer, the headset app and the account server are all in one repository, and you can run your own server.

  Builds the PC side, the headset app and the server

> cd server
> go run . -public-url https://your.server
  Your own account server is running

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no paid version, and the code is MIT licensed, so anyone can use, change and share it.

Do I need an account?

Not on your own network: open RinCast on the Quest and your PC shows up. You only need one to connect from other networks.

Does my video go through your server?

Never. The server introduces your headset and PC, and they then connect directly, using IPv6, hole punching or a router port mapping. If no direct path exists, the connection fails and tells you why. It doesn't fall back to a relay.

Which games work?

Anything that runs in SteamVR. Your installed Steam VR games are listed in the headset, and you can also start games from your PC.

Which headsets work?

Meta Quest 3 is the one RinCast is built and tested for.

How do I get the microphone working in apps?

RinCast uses Steam's streaming audio devices. Turn on Remote Play in Steam to add them, then choose Steam Streaming Microphone as the input in your app.

My Quest can't find my PC. What now?

Check that RinCast Streamer is running, that both are on the same network (guest Wi-Fi keeps them apart), and that Windows' firewall allows it on private networks. If your network blocks discovery, enter the PC's address in the headset.

How does it compare with Virtual Desktop?

Virtual Desktop is a polished paid app that supports more headsets and has features RinCast doesn't, such as frame generation and eye tracking forwarding. RinCast is free and open source, and it can use your own server. If you want the most complete option today, Virtual Desktop is worth a look.
